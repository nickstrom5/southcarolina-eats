# South Carolina brand (Nick's calls, 2026-10-26, AskUserQuestion)

- **Name:** "South Carolina Eats" (19 characters). "South Carolina Eats: Restaurants" is 32, over the App Store's 30, so the family's ": Restaurants"
  tail moves out. Subtitle "BBQ, Shrimp & Grits, Oysters" (28). Home Screen "SC Eats". Site: southcarolina.eatsranked.com.
  Other options shown: "SC Eats: Restaurants" (closest to the family pattern, weaker for search), "Palmetto Eats: Restaurants" (palmettoeats.com
  registered 2024-12 at Automattic; "Palmetto" fills SC business names). southcarolinaeats.com is registered (2026-03, Unstoppable Domains),
  which doesn't matter. No App Store app found under these names (web search, 2026-10-26; apps.apple.com and itunes.apple.com disallow
  Claude, so App Store collisions are a hand search). The USPTO lookup (tmsearch.uspto.gov) is done by hand before the App Store record.
  Avoided: "Discover South Carolina" (tourism brand), "Certified SC Grown" and "Fresh on the Menu" (SCDA programs), "Smiling Faces.
  Beautiful Places.", Clemson and Gamecock marks.
- **Icon:** concept A, a curled Lowcountry shrimp in side view, cooked coral #E8836B with a lighter back #F2A48F and a darker belly
  #C9634E, two antennae, a tail fan, four shell segments and the eye cut to the ground, on flag-inspired indigo #1B2A57.
  `scripts/icon-concepts.swift` `shrimp()`; passes `state-prompts/icon-style/check.swift` (box 0.719 x 0.710, 4 colours, coverage 24.6%).
  Rejected: B an oyster on the half shell on marsh green (weakest at 60 px), C a boiled peanut in the shell on indigo.
  Sheet: `playbook/brand/concepts-sheet.png`.
- **Palette:** "Flag & marsh": flag-inspired indigo #1B2A57 for text and headings (13.9:1 on white; the state color is indigo blue by
  law with no legal hex, so it's called flag-inspired, not official), marsh green #2F5D3A (6.8:1 on cream), oyster cream #F6F1E7
  surfaces, shrimp coral #E8836B only as a fill under indigo text (5.2:1; white on coral is 2.7:1 and fails). No Clemson orange/purple,
  no Gamecock garnet/black.
- **Header element:** a marsh-grass and tidal-creek band (indigo water, a cream creek line, marsh-green grass blades). No palmetto, seal
  or team mark.
