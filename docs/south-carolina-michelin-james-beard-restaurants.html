What counts as an honor here

Two sources, reported as facts rather than scores: the MICHELIN Guide's American South selection for 2026 (One Star, Bib Gourmand and Recommended, each labeled as exactly that), and the James Beard Foundation's Restaurant and Chef Awards (winners from any year, and 2025 and 2026 semifinalists and finalists) with its America's Classics. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place names them. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.
