South Carolina meat-and-threes & drive-ins

6 places for South Carolina's everyday table: a meat and three vegetables, soul food plates, and drive-ins that have served chili burgers since the 1940s and 1950s, each checked in fall 2026.

A meat and three

Pick a meat, then three sides from the steam table: the lunch counter that feeds South Carolina's towns. The drive-ins on this list date from the 1940s through the 1960s. Hours vary, so check before you go.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Spartanburg (2), Myrtle Beach (1), Greenwood (1), North Augusta (1), Florence (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a page names it for this food (the state tourism office's Discover South Carolina, local press or the place's own site) and something from 2025 or 2026 shows it's open: a dated page, the place's own site with its current hours when we looked, or a 2025–26 SC Department of Agriculture inspection. Its street address had to match our records. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.

On your phone, South Carolina Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
