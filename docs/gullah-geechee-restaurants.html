The Sea Islands' table

The Gullah Geechee people, descendants of West and Central Africans enslaved on the rice and cotton plantations of the coast, kept a cooking tradition of their own: rice at the center of the meal, okra soup, red rice, crab and shrimp rice, lima beans, greens and the seafood of the creeks. These family kitchens, from the Sea Islands near Beaufort to Charleston and its neighbors, keep cooking it. The list is about the food each one serves; ask each kitchen about its own story.
