## Sources (details, terms, checks and calibration: `playbook/12-sources.md`)
- **SCDA** (SC Department of Agriculture) permits and inspects every retail food establishment statewide (Regulation 61-25, since
  2024-07-01). SC Food Grades' public ArcGIS service (`pipeline/fetch_official.py`, ≤2,000 rows a request, serially): layer 0 = permits
  with points, table 4 = three years of inspections. **SCORE is empty**: show the posted grade (A/B/C) and date as official; never derive
  a score or grade. "S" = a suspension notice (place left off). PROG_CODE 206 retail food, 204 mobile, 208 institution, 211 grocery.
  Records through = fetch − 7 days; open = inspected in the 18 months before that. Nick (2026-10-26): use it in the app now; the reuse
  note to scfoodgrades@scda.sc.gov is a Gmail draft he sends.
- Not used: SCDOR ABL (search apps only; "serves alcohol" = "—"; bulk request drafted), county property values, Fresh on the Menu's
  member list (all rights reserved). Never link scdhec.gov.
- Calibration (statewide vs 20,012 open retail-food permits, 2026-10-28): Meta + Google-open 83.8%, Meta ≥0.95 80.8%, Foursquare-only 9.7%.
- MICHELIN American South 2026: 27 SC places (4 stars, **4 Bib Gourmand** → Bib filter in app and web app), `data/research/michelin.json`.

## SCDA pins and duplicates (2026-10-28, Nick approved the fix)
- SCDA's layer names the locator of each point (`Loc_name`, kept as `loc` in official.pkl). `geocode_official.py` drops ZIP, city and
  state centroids and street-range or Census points that three or more house numbers share, moves a street-range point to a
  disagreeing address point, and re-places the rest through address points and `geocode_census.py` (run it, then geocode_official
  again). Street-range and Census points are `weak`: `official_match` accepts their same-address match up to 30 km, and calibrate.py
  gives them their permit-number county. `official.ADDR_FIX` corrects a permit filed at an office address (Wade's).
- The same-address merge joins a map listing and a permit-only place of one name, address and town up to 30 km apart (the listing's pin
  wins). `build_research.py` DISPLAY/ALIAS keep a checked place's own name (Nance's, Wahoo's Fish House).
- Guards: `DataGuards.listedTwice` / `guessedPins` (unit tests) and the same checks in qa-site.py. Details: the shared notes'
  sc-eats-duplicates-2026-10-28.md.
- P1 round (2026-10-28, uncommitted): `street_key` reads "1635-A"/"1635 A Springdale" as 1635 Springdale and "HWY 9E"/"HWY 9 EAST" as
  "HWY 9 E" (Taco Station, Longs; Chili's, Camden); GENERIC gains SEAFOOD, SALAD(S), HIBACHI, AND, BY, so a food word alone no longer
  ties a permit to another business at its address; two listings sharing a permit keep the one named like it (Fonda la oaxaqueña).
  Websites: `one_url()` keeps the link before a label ("… (Order Online)") and drops two links in one field; qa-site.py and
  `DataGuards.notOneURL` check every website. The Swift `TrackingParams.tracked(in:)` mirrors tracking_params.py (after "#", in the
  path, no scheme, `_kx`). Charlie Brown Seafood left the Gullah guide: its only permit is its successor's (Family Seafood Market,
  4726 Rivers Ave); no source we may use confirms the closure yet, so its listing stays.
